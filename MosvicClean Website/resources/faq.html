How can we help?

We've put together some answers to common questions about our cleaning services and the way we work. If you don't see the answer you're looking for, feel free to contact our team.

General Questions

What services do you offer? +

Our services include general janitorial cleaning, porter services, move in/out cleaning, and window and glass cleaning. Additional services can be discussed based on the needs of your facility.

How do I request a quote? +

You can request a quote by using the "Get a Quote" button throughout our website. Our team can then learn more about your facility and the services you need.

Do you provide recurring cleaning services? +

Yes. Cleaning schedules can be designed around the needs of your facility, whether you require regular janitorial service or additional cleaning on a scheduled basis.

Can services be customized for my facility? +

Every facility has different needs. Our team can work with you to determine which services, schedule, and cleaning priorities are the best fit for your space.

Services

What is included with general janitorial service? +

General janitorial service can cover routine cleaning and maintenance throughout your facility. The exact scope can be adjusted according to the building and your priorities.

Do you offer daytime porter services? +

Yes. Porter services are designed to provide ongoing daytime attention to common areas, entrances, restrooms, spills, and other high-traffic areas.

Do you clean windows and glass? +

Yes. Window and glass cleaning can be scheduled as a standalone service or as part of a broader facility cleaning plan.

Do you offer move in/out cleaning? +

Yes. Move in/out cleaning is intended to prepare a space for its next occupants by providing a more thorough cleaning of the facility.

Industries

What types of facilities do you serve? +

Our services are designed to support a variety of facilities, including healthcare, educational, and commercial environments.

Can cleaning plans be adapted to different industries? +

Yes. Different environments have different priorities, traffic levels, and cleaning needs. Service plans can be adjusted to address the specific requirements of each facility.

Can I discuss my facility's specific needs? +

Absolutely. The best way to determine the right approach is to discuss your facility, its size, schedule, and cleaning priorities with our team.
