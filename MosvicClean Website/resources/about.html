Who We Are

About Brightline

More than a cleaning company.

Brightline Facility Care is a professional cleaning company focused on helping businesses maintain clean, welcoming, and dependable environments. We believe that a well-maintained space can make a meaningful difference in the way people work, learn, visit, and interact.

Our approach is centered around reliable service, attention to detail, and understanding the individual needs of every facility we serve. From everyday janitorial care to specialized cleaning services, our goal is to make facility maintenance simpler for the people who depend on it.

Our Core Values

Reliability

Quality

Service

Our Story

Growing with the spaces we serve.

Brightline was built with a simple idea: facility cleaning should be dependable, professional, and personal. What began as a commitment to providing quality cleaning has grown into a broader approach to facility care, supporting organizations across a variety of environments.

As we continue to grow, our focus remains on the same foundation that started it all — building trust with our clients, taking pride in our work, and creating cleaner spaces for the people who use them every day.
